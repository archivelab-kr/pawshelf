Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

PawShelf is built to keep your things on your Mac. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and how to turn sending off.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you put on the shelf stays on your Mac: links to your files, copies of the images, text and web links you drop, your clipboard history and your settings. They are saved inside the app's sandbox. We don't run servers that receive them, and we can't see them.

Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics

To find crashes and learn which features are used, the app sends anonymous reports through Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase). A report contains event names such as "items added", counts, setting values, crash details, the app version, the macOS version and the Mac model. Firebase also gives the app installation a random identifier.

Reports never include file names, folder paths, links, page titles, copied text or anything else you put on the shelf.

Sending is on when you first open the app. To turn it off, go to Settings › General and turn off "Send anonymous crash reports and usage statistics". Google describes how it handles this data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Link titles and icons

When you add a web link, the app loads that page from your Mac to show its title and icon, as a browser would. The website sees an ordinary visit. To turn this off, go to Settings › Advanced and turn off "Show link titles and icons (fetches the page)".

Words in images

Words in images are recognized with Apple's on-device text recognition. Images are not uploaded anywhere. To turn this off, go to Settings › Advanced and turn off "Search words inside images (processed on this Mac)".

Clipboard history

The clipboard history keeps your last 50 copies on your Mac. Copies that password managers mark as private are not recorded. To turn it off, open Settings and turn off "Keep a history of what you copy".

What we don't do

No accounts, no ads, no selling or sharing your data for advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. PawShelf doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording permission.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs.
