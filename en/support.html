Common questions

I can't see the tab.

Click the paw icon in the menu bar and choose Show the Tab. If the tab disappears only while an app is in full screen, go to Settings › Tab and shelf and turn off "Hide the tab while an app is in full screen".

Does PawShelf move or change my files?

Putting a file on the shelf doesn't move it. The shelf keeps a link, and the original stays where it is. Renaming an item only changes its name on the shelf. If the original is moved or deleted, the shelf shows that.

I removed something by mistake.

Open the shelf, click the ⋯ button and choose Recently Removed. The same list is in the paw icon menu in the menu bar.

Why doesn't PawShelf ask for Accessibility permission?

It notices drags through standard macOS events that need no special permission. It has no access to your keyboard or your screen.

How do I turn off crash reports and usage statistics?

Go to Settings › General and turn off "Send anonymous crash reports and usage statistics". The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent.

How do I uninstall PawShelf?

Click the paw icon in the menu bar, choose Quit PawShelf, then move PawShelf from the Applications folder to the Trash.
