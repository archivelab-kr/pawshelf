Drop it on the cat. Pick it up later.

PawShelf is a shelf for your Mac. A small character peeks out from the edge of your screen and holds files, folders, images, links and text while you switch between apps.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How it works

  1. Drop it on the tab

    Drag anything onto the character at the edge of your screen. Far from the edge? Shake the cursor while dragging, and the shelf opens right next to it.

  2. Carry on

    Switch apps, Spaces and full-screen windows. The tab stays at the edge, and your items are still there after a restart.

  3. Drag it out

    Drop items into Finder, Mail, a chat or an upload form. Select several to drag them out together.

Choose who keeps your things

Twelve characters, each with its own way of holding on to things. Some habits do real work: the hamster's cheeks show how full the shelf is, the squirrel points out forgotten items, and the penguin's ice melts away items you no longer need.

  • Box Cat

    Hides your things in a cardboard box

  • Pouch Hamster

    Cheeks puff up as the shelf fills

  • Acorn Squirrel

    Remembers forgotten acorns and suggests a cleanup

  • Ice Penguin

    Keeps things on ice until they melt away

  • Night Owl

    Wide awake at night, sleepy by day

  • Shiny Magpie

    Collects shiny links and pictures

  • Fetch Dog

    Fetches your things back when you need them

  • Pocket Kangaroo

    Keeps things safe in its pouch

  • Pelican

    Opens its big beak to catch drops

  • Eight-arm Octopus

    Holds one thing in each arm

  • Burrow Mole

    Stores things in its burrow

  • Shell Turtle

    Keeps things safe under its shell

Everything a shelf should do

  • Clipboard history

    Your last 50 copies sit right on the shelf. Copies that password managers mark as private are never recorded.

  • Collect new files

    Choose a folder such as Screenshots or Downloads, and new files land on the shelf by themselves.

  • Search inside images

    Find a screenshot by the words in it. Text is recognized on your Mac.

  • Link titles and icons

    Links show the page's title and icon, not just an address.

  • Recently Removed

    Removed something by mistake? Bring it back from the ⋯ menu on the shelf.

  • Preview and share

    Press Space for Quick Look, or send items with AirDrop and Messages.

  • Collections

    Group related items and show one collection at a time.

  • On every Space

    The tab follows you to every Space and sits above full-screen apps, on the left or right edge.

  • Ready for VoiceOver

    Every button and item on the shelf has a VoiceOver label.

Nothing to allow in System Settings

PawShelf doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording permission. It runs in the App Sandbox and only opens what you hand it. What you put on the shelf stays on your Mac.
